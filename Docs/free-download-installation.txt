MacPearl experimental beta — installation

Requires Apple silicon, a working Secure Enclave, and macOS 15 or later. Individual OS/device combinations
still require testing. This build is ad-hoc signed, without an Apple-verified
publisher identity or Apple notarization.

1. Open the DMG and drag MacPearl to Applications.
2. Eject the DMG. Open MacPearl from Applications.
3. If macOS blocks it as an unidentified developer or says Apple cannot check
   it for malicious software, open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   If offered, choose Open Anyway for MacPearl and confirm the system prompt.
   Managed Macs may prevent this override.
   If macOS reports malware or damage, stop and contact the download provider.
4. Complete setup: Camera permission, Accessibility permission, face enrollment
   and Store and Authorize for your Mac password. Use Authorize Session again
   after quitting or restarting MacPearl.

Keep macOS security protections enabled. No Terminal bypass is needed in these
instructions. Only approve a download obtained from the intended provider.

Recognition runs locally. The beta has no liveness detection; resistance to
photographs and video replay has not been established. It is a convenience
feature for an already signed-in session, not Face ID, FileVault or startup login.
Keep your password available for manual unlocking.

Updates are manual: pause automatic unlock, quit MacPearl, then replace the app
in Applications with the newer download. Ad-hoc signatures can change between
builds. Camera/Accessibility permissions may need to be granted again, and
Keychain authorization or re-enrollment may be needed. Upgrade behavior must be
tested for each release; do not assume a seamless migration from development
or Developer ID builds.

Before uninstalling: remove the stored password and saved face in MacPearl's
Settings, turn off launch at login, quit, then delete the app from Applications.
Deleting the app alone does not remove its Keychain data. Camera/Accessibility
permissions can be removed separately in System Settings.

Credential protection in this build: macOS user presence is required for a Secure
Enclave key operation. Only a device-wrapped key, public key and encrypted password
are persisted in the traditional Keychain; the session encryption key is not stored
in plaintext. Older builds with Keychain error -34018 must be replaced. Earlier
credential services are not migrated automatically; set up credentials again.
